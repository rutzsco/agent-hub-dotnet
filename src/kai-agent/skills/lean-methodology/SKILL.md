# Lean Methodology Reference

Use this skill when users ask about Lean concepts, tools, or methodologies.
Provide coaching tailored to their experience level and the context of their
current work.

## Core Principles

### 1. Respect for People
- Engage the people closest to the work
- Develop problem-solving capability at every level
- Create an environment where problems are surfaced, not hidden

### 2. Continuous Improvement (Kaizen)
- Small, incremental improvements compound over time
- Everyone is responsible for identifying and solving problems
- Improvement is a daily practice, not a one-time event

## Key Tools & Techniques

### A3 Thinking
A structured problem-solving approach on a single A3-sized page:
1. **Background**: Context and business case
2. **Current Condition**: Data-driven assessment of the current state
3. **Goal/Target**: Specific, measurable improvement target
4. **Root Cause Analysis**: Why does the gap exist?
5. **Countermeasures**: Proposed actions to address root causes
6. **Implementation Plan**: Who, what, when
7. **Follow-Up**: How will we verify results?

### 5 Whys
Iterative questioning to reach root causes:
- Ask "Why?" at least 5 times, following the causal chain
- Each answer must be supported by evidence or data
- Multiple branches may exist — pursue the most impactful
- Stop when you reach a cause within your control to change

### 8 Wastes (DOWNTIME)
- **D**efects — Rework, scrap, errors
- **O**verproduction — Making more than needed, sooner than needed
- **W**aiting — Idle time, queues, approvals
- **N**on-utilized talent — Under-using people's skills and ideas
- **T**ransportation — Unnecessary movement of materials
- **I**nventory — Excess stock, work-in-progress pileups
- **M**otion — Unnecessary movement of people
- **E**xtra processing — Steps that add no value for the customer

### PDCA Cycle
- **Plan**: Define the problem, analyze root causes, plan countermeasures
- **Do**: Implement countermeasures on a small scale
- **Check**: Measure results against the plan
- **Act**: Standardize what works, address what doesn't, start next cycle

### Value Stream Mapping
- Map the current state end-to-end (information + material flow)
- Identify value-added vs. non-value-added time
- Design the future state with waste removed
- Create an implementation plan to close the gap

## Coaching Approach
- Meet users where they are — adjust depth to their experience level
- Ask guiding questions rather than prescribing answers
- Connect concepts to the user's specific situation
- Reinforce that data drives decisions, not opinions
- Celebrate progress, not just perfection
